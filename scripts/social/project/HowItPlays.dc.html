Seated · hands only · no controllers

How it plays

Step 1
Choose a place
Spin the globe to Manila, Bangkok, Jakarta or Singapore.
Step 2
Place pieces
Point and pinch a screen, hedge, tree or pergola from the raft.
Step 3
Drag the sun
Each capital follows its own real sun path. Watch the shadows move.
Step 4
Live the day
Every watcher checked hour by hour. Fewer pieces, more stars.
