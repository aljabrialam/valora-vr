# Submission form (draft)

**Submission name:** VaLora: Two Watchers

**Tagline (under 140 characters):** Same sun, different skies. A seated, hands-only VR puzzle: let the sun in and keep the watchers out across South-east Asia.

**Track:** Gaming

**Division:** New Experience (to confirm with the organisers; see [COMPETITION](COMPETITION.md))

**Description (draft, under 500 words):**

*Inspiration.* Every tropical house fights the same battles: letting light in, keeping eyes out, staying dry in the monsoon and sheltered from the typhoon. We noticed they are all one question, can this watcher reach that spot in a straight line?, and built a game around it. The sun is just a watcher very far away.

*What it is.* A seated, hands-only puzzle. A small island floats in front of you with a house, its courtyard and the neighbours. Pick a country on a globe: Manila, Bangkok, Jakarta or Singapore, each with its own house, its own sun path for the day, and its own twist: a neighbour at the window, long-tail boats on the canal, a high-rise looking down, typhoon wind, monsoon rain. Point and pinch screens, hedges, trees and pergolas from a bamboo raft onto the courtyard, drag the sun across the sky, then live the day and watch every rule checked hour by hour. Beams show each watcher's line of sight and stop on whatever blocks it. Tiki, a house gecko on the garden wall, teaches by doing. Fewer pieces earn more stars.

*How we built it.* Meta's Immersive Web SDK and WebXR in Quest Browser. One small geometry engine answers "can this watcher reach that spot?" for every watcher, and the same code proves all 12 levels solvable and finds the fewest pieces each needs. The islands and houses are generated by script in Blender and built never to contradict the checks: what you see is what is checked. Every action is a ray pinch, so it works with hand tracking. We tested with simulated hands in Meta's IWER emulator, plus a borrowed Quest.

*What's next.* A daily challenge with the real sun path for today's date, and more of ASEAN on the globe.

*Note:* illustrative sunlight and visibility checks, not certified daylight, comfort, weather or privacy analysis.

**Hand interactions:** every action is a ray pinch: pick a piece from the raft, point it onto the courtyard, pinch it to turn it, drag the sun, press play. No controller is needed at any point, and the view is set up once for a seated player.
