# Controls

## In the headset (hands)

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Enter | Select **Enter VR** on the page, then put the controllers down |
| Swing the privacy screen | Point at the bronze handle on the screen's end, pinch, move your hand around the hinge, let go |
| Slide the overhang | Pinch the handle on its edge and pull or push (*planned*) |
| Move the sun | Pinch the sun and drag it along its arc (*planned*) |
| See through a watcher's eyes | Pinch and hold the neighbour's window or the sun (*planned*) |
| Undo, reset, pause | Turn your left palm up to open the wrist menu, pinch a button (*planned*) |

## In the emulator (IWER)

Open the dev URL, select **Enter XR**, then in the IWER toolbar choose **Select input mode → Gaze + Hands**.

| Emulated action | Mouse and keys |
|---|---|
| Aim | Move the pointer |
| Pinch | Hold the left mouse button |
| Move the pinching hand | Drag while holding the left button |
| Move the hand nearer or further | Mouse wheel while pinching |
| Turn your head | Drag with the right mouse button |
| Reset the hand | Space |

Source: [IWSDK testing guide](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/iwsdk/guides/02-testing-experience/).
