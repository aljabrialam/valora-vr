# Borrowed Quest test checklist

Use a Quest 3 or 3S with hand tracking on and controllers put away. Sit at a normal chair. Record results in [TESTING](TESTING.md) under *Headset*.

## Before you start
- [ ] Quest Browser updated; hand tracking enabled in Settings
- [ ] Open the judge URL over HTTPS; Enter VR appears
- [ ] Session starts without a controller ever being paired or picked up

## Comfort and reach (seated)
- [ ] The desk is at a comfortable height; nothing needs standing or leaning
- [ ] Every handle is reachable within a two-foot radius
- [ ] No arm fatigue after 5 minutes of play
- [ ] Watcher views and the seat view fade gently; no discomfort

## Hands
- [ ] Ray pinch reliably grabs the screen handle on the first try (10 attempts: __/10)
- [ ] Dragging feels connected; detents click and do not jitter between two angles
- [ ] Releasing leaves the screen where expected
- [ ] Overhang and sun handles behave the same way
- [ ] Menu (undo, reset, pause) opens and works with hands only

## Readability
- [ ] Labels SEEN / PRIVATE / IN SUN / SHADED readable at arm's length
- [ ] Level card readable without leaning in
- [ ] Meaning is clear without relying on colour

## Performance
- [ ] Stable frame rate (target 72 fps or higher; judging minimum 60)
- [ ] No hitches when beams update or labels redraw
- [ ] Load time from link to Enter VR: ___ s

## Full run
- [ ] Complete tutorial → three levels → seat view with hands only, in under 10 minutes
- [ ] Pause and resume (take the headset off and on) restores the game
- [ ] No console errors (connect over USB and check, or use `metavr log`)
