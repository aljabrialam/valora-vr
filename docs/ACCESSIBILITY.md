# Accessibility and comfort

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Seated by design; everything within a two-foot radius | Works in an airplane seat, a wheelchair or a sofa |
| Playable with one hand, either hand | Every action is a single pinch and drag |
| Ray pinch on every target | Works with hand tracking, controllers, and look-and-pinch on Meta VR Glasses |
| Pieces snap to sockets; a short pinch turns them; invisible hit boxes larger than thin parts | Forgiving of tracking jitter and tremor; nothing needs precise aim |
| Meaning in words and symbols, not colour alone | SEEN / PRIVATE with an eye symbol, SUN / SHADE with the hour, DRY / WET, SHELTERED / WINDY, check and cross on the level card |
| No locomotion; the view is set once for a seated player | Avoids vection and motion sickness |
| No time pressure | Puzzles wait for you; the day only plays when you press play |
| Playable without a headset | The same game runs in a browser with a mouse or touch, with the words at reading size |
| Sound confirms every snap | Hands have no vibration; audio stands in for haptics, and every sound has a visual twin |
